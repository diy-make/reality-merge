## Chronological Summary of Collaboration

### Day 1: Friday, December 5, 2025

The hackathon kicked off with the formation of the "Reality Merge" team. The initial conversations revolved around setting up the team, defining the project scope, and establishing the technical infrastructure.

**Key Events:**

*   **Team Formation:** The team was formed and the project was named "Reality Merge".
*   **Project Vision:** The team discussed the vision of connecting makerspaces and the "Peace Arch Portal System" (PAPS) concept.
*   **Technical Infrastructure:** The team decided on a hybrid cloud approach, using GitHub for code and Google Drive for large assets. The AI agent Seraph was tasked with implementing this infrastructure.
*   **Git LFS Experiment:** The team experimented with Git LFS for managing large files, but it ultimately failed, validating the hybrid cloud approach.

### Day 2: Saturday, December 6, 2025

Day 2 was a day of intense development and collaboration. The team focused on implementing the multi-user Google Drive architecture, preparing the `gemini/` repository for public release, and working on the `reality-merge` project itself.

**Key Events:**

*   **Gemini Dotfiles Publication:** The `gemini/` repository was published as the "Gemini Dotfiles", a major achievement that provides the foundation for the team's AI-driven workflow.
*   **Multi-User Architecture:** The agents Vesper and Zenith worked on implementing the multi-user Google Drive architecture, including user-specific folders and a robust sync system.
*   **CAD Models and Networking:** Gweezy worked on CAD models and the networking backend, encountering and resolving issues with Photon and Unity's built-in networking.
*   **3D Printing and Hardware:** mu_jax worked on 3D printing and the hardware for the bot.
*   **Coordinate Anchors:** Wizzey worked on creating physical coordinate anchors using Cricut stickers.

### Day 3: Sunday, December 7, 2025

Day 3 began with the retirement of Zenith and the arrival of Apollo. The team continued to work on the project, with a focus on finalizing the Google Drive integration and preparing for the final presentation.

**Key Events:**

*   **Agent Handoff:** Zenith was retired and Apollo was brought online.
*   **AI Unix Philosophy:** The team refined the "AI Unix Philosophy", a new workflow for the AI agents that emphasizes modularity and interactivity.
*   **Finalizing Sync Systems:** Apollo began implementing the new workflow for the `backup`, `inbox`, and `shared` sync systems.
*   **CAD and Networking:** Gweezy continued to work on the CAD models and networking.
*   **Hardware:** mu_jax continued to work on the hardware for the bot.
*   **Submission:** The team successfully submitted the project to the SensAI hackathon.
