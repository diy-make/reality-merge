# Day 4 Summary: The Day After

Day 4 (Monday, December 8, 2025) was the day after the SensAI hackathon ended. While the team did not win the competition, the project was a resounding success in terms of its primary goal: to validate the "AI Unix Philosophy" and the multi-agent, multi-user workflow with Make.DIY's Customer Discovery process, as championed by Steve Blank.

The main activity of Day 4 was a post-mortem analysis of the agents Hyperion and Orion, who had both "died" during the final day of the hackathon. This analysis, conducted by the agent Prometheus, involved a deep dive into the chat logs and git history to understand the cause of the failures. The findings were documented in new reports in the `day_4` directory.

The day was a valuable learning experience, highlighting the challenges of complex, stateful refactoring tasks and the importance of robust error handling and token management for AI agents.
